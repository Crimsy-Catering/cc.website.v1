Privacy notice.

This is a starter outline only. It is not a complete legal privacy notice. Replace the placeholders and make sure the finished policy accurately reflects your website and how you handle data.

Review before launch

Who is responsible for your information?

Add the legal name or trading name of the business, its contact details and the appropriate privacy contact.

What information may be collected?

For example, the information submitted in a quotation form, such as your name, email address, phone number, event date, venue location and event requirements.

How will it be used?

Explain how you use enquiry details to respond to requests, prepare quotations, manage confirmed bookings and meet relevant legal obligations.

Who receives the information?

List the website host, form provider, email provider and other services that actually process enquiry information on your behalf.

How long is it kept?

Set out realistic retention periods or the criteria used to decide how long information is retained.

Your rights and contact

Explain applicable data protection rights and how people can contact you with a request or complaint. Make sure the notice reflects UK data protection requirements and your actual practices.

Cookies and analytics

Document the cookies and analytics tools you use. If you add non-essential tracking, implement an appropriate consent mechanism before those technologies run where required.

This page is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Review it against the actual tools and practices used on your live site.
