Booking information & policies.

Use this page to explain how enquiries, quotations and confirmed bookings work. The sample structure below must be reviewed and completed with your actual policies before launch.

Complete before publishing

What customers need to know.

1. Enquiries and quotations

Submitting a quotation request does not reserve a date or confirm a booking. A booking is confirmed only after Crimsy Catering has checked availability and communicated the agreed terms and next steps.

2. Package inclusions and capacity

Set out the confirmed duration, food and service inclusions, guest capacity, setup and pack-down arrangements and any venue requirements for each package. Do not publish unverified guest limits.

3. Deposits and payments

Business owner to complete: state whether a deposit is required, the amount, when the balance is due, accepted payment methods and what happens if payment is late.

4. Cancellations and rescheduling

Business owner to complete: explain how customers can cancel or request a date change, the relevant deadlines and any applicable refund or rescheduling terms. Have the policy checked for legal compliance.

5. Travel, access and venue arrangements

Business owner to complete: list your service area, travel charges if any, access and parking requirements, setup time and any power or space requirements.

6. Allergens and dietary information

Customers should ask for accurate ingredient and allergen information before booking. Explain your actual preparation procedures and cross-contact risks. Never promise that an item is allergen-free unless your procedures support that claim.

7. Photography and reviews

Only publish customer reviews and event photographs where you have permission. Obtain appropriate consent before using identifiable images of guests.

8. Contact

Add your business email and a suitable way for customers to contact you about a booking.
